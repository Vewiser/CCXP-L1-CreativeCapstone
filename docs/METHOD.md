# The CCXP Method

## 1. Notice

Write the observed behavior or friction without prescribing a solution.

## 2. Frame

Name the person, moment, desired change, constraints, and evidence of success.

## 3. Make

Choose the fastest artifact capable of testing the riskiest assumption.

## 4. Test

Watch what people do. Ask what confused them, what changed, and what they would use or pay for.

## 5. Learn

Separate facts, interpretations, and decisions. Record what failed while it is still fresh.

## 6. Package

Turn the proven pieces into a clearer service, product, template, lesson, or system.

## Quality gate

Before publishing, ask: Is it true? Is it useful? Is it clearly mine? Is the audience respected? Can I show the evidence?
