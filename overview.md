# Overview

CCXP stands for **Creative Conscious Experience Project**. It is the entry-level operating system behind V. Dixon's practice: see clearly, build deliberately, test in real life, and preserve what was learned.

## Who it serves

- Creative entrepreneurs who need a path from idea to offer.
- Small teams that need clear systems without corporate weight.
- Designers and technologists building human-centered experiments.
- Parents, educators, and creators turning curiosity into projects.

## What makes it different

The framework joins three things that are often separated:

- **Creative direction:** taste, story, identity, and experience.
- **Systems thinking:** inputs, decisions, workflows, and measurement.
- **Conscious practice:** attention, ethics, reflection, and human impact.

## Proof over performance

The repository should show completed decisions and lessons—not folders that merely imply work exists. Every listed deliverable must be present, marked as planned, or removed.
