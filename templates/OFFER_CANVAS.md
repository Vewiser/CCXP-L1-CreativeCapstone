# Offer Canvas

- **Person:** Who is already feeling this problem?
- **Problem:** What costly or frustrating situation exists now?
- **Promise:** What believable change will the offer create?
- **Proof:** Why should they trust the method?
- **Process:** What are the 3–5 delivery steps?
- **Package:** What exactly is included and excluded?
- **Price:** What fixed pilot price matches the risk and value?
- **Path:** What is the smallest next action?
- **Profit check:** Delivery time, hard cost, margin, and reusable assets.
